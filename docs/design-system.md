# Rotean design system

## Direction
Rotean should feel considered, warm and quietly intelligent. It should not resemble a generic AI dashboard.

## Palette
- Paper: #F7F5EF
- Surface: #FFFEFA
- Ink: #17201B
- Muted ink: #68736C
- Forest: #234D3A
- Deep forest: #173729
- Lime: #B8D85B
- Accent yellow/orange/red used sparingly for meaning, not decoration.

Avoid purple, blue and cyan as primary brand colors.

## Typography
Use a strong editorial display face for major brand moments and a highly legible sans-serif for product UI. Keep hierarchy obvious and copy human.

## Interaction
Motion should explain state: press feedback, screen transitions, expanding details, and schedule changes. Avoid perpetual floating animations, excessive gradients, glassmorphism, and decorative motion.

## Content
Prefer plain language: “You have 75 minutes” over “Available time detected”. Prefer “Want me to move this?” over “Confirm rescheduling action”.

## Components
Build from primitives first: Screen, Text styles, Button, Card, Row, SectionTitle, Input, Chip, TimelineItem, BottomSheet. Components should be composable and domain-agnostic where possible.
